How the Backyard Ultra works

One 5 km loop, every hour, until one runner remains.

Saturday, October 10, 2026 · Yard 1 starts at 10:00 am ET · Yale SOM, 165 Whitney Avenue

5 kmper yard
60 minto finish it
1finisher

Format

1

Every hour, on the hour, all remaining runners start a 5 km loop, called a yard. 20 yards is 100 km.

2

Finish the yard within the hour and you can rest until the next start. Finish fast and you rest longer.

3

You must be in the starting corral when the bell rings. Miss a start, or fail to finish a yard inside the hour, and you are out.

4

The race ends when one runner completes a yard alone. That runner is the winner. Everyone else is a DNF, however far they went. The runner-up is the assist.

5

Supporters pledge per yard, so every loop a runner completes raises more for the cause.

Loop overview

Every yard starts and finishes at Yale SOM, Evans Hall, 165 Whitney Avenue. The course changes twice during the day.

North on Whitney Avenue, across to the Farmington Canal trail on Canal Street, down Ivy Path to Phelps Triangle, and back to the corral. Roughly 85% paved.

Run once, at 6 pm. Combines the canal section of the day loop with the northern block of the night loop, so runners see the night course in daylight. Roughly 75% paved.

Run this drawn loop twice. The night route shown is 2.5 km, so each yard is two laps of it.

From yard 10 onward. Up Whitney Avenue, along the edge of East Rock Park by the Mill River, and back by Everit Street. Roughly 60% paved, so bring a headlamp.
