How the Backyard Ultra works

One 5 km loop, every hour, until one runner remains.

Saturday, October 10, 2026 · Yard 1 starts at 10:00 am ET · 90 William St, New Haven

5 kmper yard
60 minto finish it
1finisher

Format

1

Every hour, on the hour, all remaining runners start a 5 km loop, called a yard. 20 yards is 100 km.

2

Finish the yard within the hour and you can rest until the next start. Finish fast and you rest longer.

3

You must be in the starting corral when the bell rings. Miss a start, or fail to finish a yard inside the hour, and you are out.

4

The race ends when one runner completes a yard alone. That runner is the winner. Everyone else is a DNF, however far they went. The runner-up is the assist.

5

Supporters pledge per yard, so every loop a runner completes raises more for the cause.

Route overview

Five routes over the day. Every yard is 5 km, and the corral moves twice, so check which route is on before each start.

  • 10 am – 6 pm90 William St
  • 6 – 10 pm252 Willow St
  • 10 pm on169 Livingston St

Day route · 10 am – 5 pm · Yards 1–7

Corral: 90 William St

A long loop through East Rock: up Orange Street, around by the edge of East Rock Park, and back down State Street. About 64% paved.

Transition 1 · 5 – 6 pm · Yard 8

Starts: 90 William St  →  Finishes: 252 Willow St

This yard finishes in a different place from where it starts. It ends at the evening corral, 252 Willow St.

Run once, at 5 pm. It leaves from the day corral, heads up State Street and past East Rock Park, comes down Whitney Avenue and finishes at the evening corral. About 65% paved.

Pre Drinks route · 6 – 9 pm · Yards 9–11

Corral: 252 Willow St

The evening loop while pre-drinks are on: out along Whitney Avenue, up to the edge of East Rock Park, and back past College Woods. About 53% paved.

Transition 2 · 9 – 10 pm · Yard 12

Starts: 252 Willow St  →  Finishes: 169 Livingston St

This yard finishes in a different place from where it starts. It ends at the night corral, 169 Livingston St.

Run once, at 9 pm. Similar to the Pre Drinks route, with an unpaved stretch along the park, finishing at the night corral. Bring a headlamp.

Night route · 10 pm on · Yards 13+ · 2 laps

Corral: 169 Livingston St

Run this drawn loop twice. The night route shown is 2.5 km, so each yard is two laps of it.

From 10 pm until the last runner is standing. Up Whitney Avenue, along the edge of East Rock Park by the Mill River, and back by Everit Street. About 60% paved, so bring a headlamp.
